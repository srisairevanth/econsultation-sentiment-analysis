Truth in Lending Act (TILA) / Regulation Z (Overview)

The Truth in Lending Act requires lenders to disclose credit terms and costs to consumers in a
standardized, understandable way, so borrowers can compare loan offers. It is implemented
primarily through Regulation Z, issued by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.

Commonly referenced concepts:
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR): a standardized measure of the cost of credit.
- Right of rescission: for certain loans secured by a consumer's principal dwelling, borrowers
  may cancel within a specified period.
- Required disclosures for credit cards, mortgages, and other consumer credit products.
- Rules limiting certain high-cost mortgage practices.
